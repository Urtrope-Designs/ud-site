I am a self-taught web developer, with an academic background in Latin, Medieval Studies, and Linguistics. Professionally, I have spent the past 15 years on the client side of web tech, turning ideas into working software by getting something real in front of users as quickly as possible and letting their feedback shape it.

Game development is what drove me to programming, and something I still love to pursue - an artistic expression that is maleable and infinitely interactive. 

Ultimately I just love exploring the human experience through the stuff we create, whether that's philosophy or music or post-human technology.
